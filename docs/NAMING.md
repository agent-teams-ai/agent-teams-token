# Token naming research

Status: product decision required. These are working names, not trademark
clearance.

1. **Agent Teams AI (`ATAI`) - best brand match, but collision must be accepted.
   🎯 10/10 🛡️ 5/10 🧠 1/10, roughly 20-40 LOC now; 100-250 LOC if renamed
   after integrations exist.** Exact symbol is already used by
   [ArtemisAI](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/artemisai) and by SEC registrant
   [AtaiBeckley Inc.](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=2081043&owner=exclude).
   A symbol is not globally unique, but users must always verify the chain and
   contract address, and search/listing confusion will remain.
2. **Agent Teams Commons (`ATCM`) - safest community-oriented option. 🎯 9/10
   🛡️ 8/10 🧠 2/10, roughly 25-45 LOC now; 100-250 LOC after integrations.**
   "Commons" communicates stewardship without promising profit. The 2026-08-27
   CoinGecko/SEC exact-symbol check found no match, but this is not clearance.
3. **Agent Teams (`AGTM`) - compact neutral alternative. 🎯 8/10 🛡️ 8/10 🧠
   1/10, roughly 20-40 LOC now; 100-250 LOC after integrations.** It stays close
   to the organization name and had no exact CoinGecko/SEC result on 2026-08-27,
   but is less immediately recognisable than `ATAI`.

Before a public launch, perform formal trademark, exchange-symbol, domain and
social-handle clearance in the launch jurisdictions. Naming must never imply
equity, revenue share, guaranteed utility or future price appreciation.
