# Token naming research

Status: product decision required. These are working names, not trademark
clearance.

1. **Agent Teams Commons (`ATCM`) - recommended. 🎯 9/10 🛡️ 8/10 🧠 2/10,
   roughly 25-45 LOC now; 100-250 LOC if renamed after integrations exist.**
   "Commons" communicates community stewardship without promising profit.
2. **Agent Teams Collective (`ATCL`). 🎯 8/10 🛡️ 8/10 🧠 2/10, roughly
   25-45 LOC now; 100-250 LOC after integrations.** Strong community meaning,
   but less directly tied to the product name.
3. **Agent Teams AI (`ATMAI`). 🎯 8/10 🛡️ 7/10 🧠 1/10, roughly 20-40 LOC
   now; 100-250 LOC after integrations.** The clearest product association,
   but a more generic and collision-prone name.

Avoid `ATAI`, `AGTM`, `TEAMAI` and `ATMC`: research found existing projects,
DEX symbols or historical marks close enough to create unnecessary confusion.

Before a public launch, perform formal trademark, exchange-symbol, domain and
social-handle clearance in the launch jurisdictions. Naming must never imply
equity, revenue share, guaranteed utility or future price appreciation.
