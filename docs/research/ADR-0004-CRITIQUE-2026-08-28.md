# ADR-0004 independent critique, 2026-08-28

Status: three hosted subscription-runtime critics completed.

## Verdict

All three reviews returned the same practical decision: accept the two-context
direction only after amendments. Do not combine ADR acceptance with immediate
package migration.

## Corrections incorporated

- `Token Control` owns economic authorization and immutable transfer intent;
  adapters do not own policy.
- `Cross-chain Accounting` owns transfer identity, evidence, idempotency,
  duplicate/conflict detection, finality/reorg transitions, reconciliation and
  incident classification. Aggregate counters are projections, not authority.
- The handoff between contexts is an immutable versioned intent artifact with
  allocation, amount, route/configuration binding, controller and expiry.
- Neither context implements Chainlink CCIP, a relayer, token pool or Solana
  program.
- Accepting the ADR records target architecture. Migration follows later after
  Genesis Core, topology gates and artifact-identity protection are ready.
- Feature ownership applies to production behavior without forcing package
  metadata, schemas, ABI declarations and composition into artificial folders.
- ADR-0003 lifecycle history is superseded atomically only after explicit owner
  approval; its surviving Clean Architecture constraints remain in ADR-0004.

## Separate product decision

`NoCatchUpVesting` is not part of ADR-0004. It is a token-allocation policy that
remains blocked on approved beneficiary groups, allocation sizes, start dates,
release limits and recovery rules. It controls access to already minted tokens;
it never creates additional supply.
